write me a story post about how I ended up doing bookkeeping. here's what happened:

- december 2018 was our best month ever at the café, fully booked every weekend
- I took that money and bought a second coffee machine in january, £4,800
- january and february were dead quiet, takings halved
- I didn't have a cash forecast, I only looked at the bank balance
- in february I couldn't pay the fish supplier on time. he stopped delivering for 2 weeks
- I borrowed £6,000 from my brother to cover wages in march
- we closed in april 2019. I sold the machines and the fridges for £3,200
- the thing that still bugs me: the numbers were all there in my receipts box, I just never added them up weekly
- in 2020 I trained as a bookkeeper
- my first client was the pizza place two doors down from where the café was
