# Attribute checklists by domain

Running letters against attributes, instead of the whole subject, is what turns generic answers into specific ones. For each subject, pick the 4–7 attributes that matter most. Don't use the whole list.

## Content and marketing (campaigns, posts, newsletters, launches)
- **Core message or angle.** What we claim and why anyone should care.
- **Audience or persona.** Who it's for, and who it's accidentally for.
- **Format.** Article, video, carousel, quiz, tool, event, email, podcast, print.
- **Channel and placement.** Where it appears, and where people meet it in their day.
- **Messenger.** Brand, founder, author, customer, employee, influencer, critic.
- **Hook.** First line, first frame, subject line, thumbnail.
- **Structure.** Order of the beats, length, series vs one-off.
- **Offer and CTA.** What we ask for and what they get.
- **Proof.** Data, testimonial, demo, guarantee, social proof.
- **Timing and cadence.** Moment, season, frequency, sequence.
- **Visual identity.** Imagery, palette, typography, motion.
- **Measurement.** What success looks like.

Setup tips:
- Note what competitors and the category usually do. It feeds Adapt and Reverse.
- Note what the audience hates about the category. It feeds Eliminate.

## Writing

### Fiction
- **Premise and genre.**
- **Protagonist.** Their want, their need, their flaw.
- **Antagonist or opposing force.**
- **Point of view and narrator.** Who tells it, and how reliable they are.
- **Setting.** Place, era, world rules.
- **Structure.** Chronology, acts, timelines, framing device.
- **Stakes and ticking clock.**
- **Inciting incident, midpoint, ending.**
- **Theme.**
- **Voice, tone and style.**
- **Medium or form.** Novel, epistolary, oral history, serial, interactive.

### Non-fiction, articles and essays
- **Thesis or angle.**
- **Lede or opening.**
- **Structure.** Listicle, narrative, Q&A, case study, timeline.
- **Evidence.** Data, anecdote, interview, expert.
- **Examples.**
- **Voice and register.**
- **Length and depth.**
- **Headline and dek.**
- **Visuals and sidebars.**
- **Ending and takeaway.**

### Copy (ads, landing pages, product descriptions, blurbs, headlines)
- **Headline.**
- **Promise or benefit.**
- **Audience pain point.**
- **Proof.**
- **Objection handling.**
- **Tone.**
- **Length.**
- **CTA.**
- **Word choice.** Abstract vs concrete.
- **Structure.** Problem → agitate → solve, before/after, story.

Setup tip for writing: keep the original text in view. In apply-and-produce mode, show each variant next to the original so the change is visible.

## Visual, UI and graphic design
- **Layout and grid.** Hierarchy, reading pattern.
- **Typography.**
- **Colour.**
- **Imagery and illustration.**
- **Navigation and information architecture.**
- **Inputs and controls.** Forms, buttons, gestures.
- **States.** Empty, loading, error, success, first use.
- **Flow and steps.** Onboarding, checkout, signup.
- **Content density.**
- **Motion and feedback.**
- **Brand elements.** Logo, voice in microcopy.
- **Device and context.** Mobile, desktop, print, out-of-home, accessibility.

Setup tips:
- Note the user's job-to-be-done on this screen or piece.
- Usability-test any Reverse or Eliminate idea before committing.
- For print and graphic work (book covers, posters), swap the interface attributes for:
  - format and size;
  - focal image;
  - type treatment;
  - colour;
  - texture, finish and material;
  - where it's seen (shelf, thumbnail, wall).

## Product, business and process
- **Product or service:**
  - core function;
  - components and features;
  - materials;
  - price and business model;
  - packaging;
  - distribution;
  - users and buyers;
  - usage context;
  - support;
  - end of life.
- **Process:**
  - steps and their order;
  - roles and handoffs;
  - inputs and outputs;
  - rules and approvals;
  - tools;
  - timing and frequency;
  - batch size;
  - location;
  - the metrics that drive behaviour.

Setup tips:
- Map the current process or customer journey first, then run SCAMPER on its most painful steps.
- If the root cause of a failure is unknown, run 5 Whys or a fishbone diagram first.

## Publishing (books and literature)
- **Title, cover and blurb.**
- **Format.** Hardback, paperback, ebook, audio, serial, illustrated, special edition.
- **Price and bundle.**
- **Release timing and sequence.**
- **Launch events.**
- **Author platform.**
- **Reader community.**
- **Retail and library channels.**
- **Backlist.**
- **Rights and adaptations.** Translation, film, merchandise.
- **Reading-group material.**
- **Review and ARC strategy.**

## Typical limits (for the map step)

When the source doesn't fix a limit, use these. They are common conventions and rules of thumb, not platform guarantees. If the user names a size or length, theirs wins.

**Visual sizes (px)**
| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Instagram / LinkedIn portrait post, carousel slide | 1080×1350 (4:5) |
| Square post | 1080×1080 |
| Story / Reel / TikTok cover | 1080×1920 (9:16) |
| LinkedIn / X landscape image | 1200×627 / 1600×900 |
| Pinterest pin | 1000×1500 (2:3) |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280×720 |
| Email body width | 600 |
| Link preview (Open Graph) image | 1200×630 |
| Landing page hero | 1440 wide desktop; check at 390 phone width |

**Copy lengths (rules of thumb)**
| Slot | Keep it to |
|---|---|
| Headline / infographic title | 3–10 words, ≤2 lines at display size |
| Tagline / subhead | 6–12 words |
| Email subject line | ~40–50 characters, so it isn't cut off on phones |
| Email preview text | ~40–90 characters |
| Card or column bullets | 3–5 per card, ≤6 words each |
| Stage or step labels | 1–2 words |
| Button / CTA | 2–4 words, verb first |
| Social caption hook | The first line, ≤~125 characters, before the "more" cut-off |
| Meta description | ~150–160 characters |
| Back-cover blurb | ~100–200 words |

**Reformat targets (rules of thumb)**
| Format | Shape |
|---|---|
| Carousel (LinkedIn / Instagram) | 6–10 slides: hook slide, one idea per slide, ≤25 words per slide, save-worthy last slide with CTA |
| Thread (X / LinkedIn) | 5–8 posts; the first must stand alone; one idea per post; CTA last; X posts ≤280 characters |
| Short video script (Reels / Shorts / TikTok) | 30–60 s; hook in the first 2 s; ~2.5 spoken words per second; on-screen text ≤7 words per beat |
| Newsletter section | 150–300 words, one idea, one link |
| Quote cards | 1 line each, ≤20 words, attributed |
| Podcast talking points | 3–5 points, each with one story or example |

**Infographic density:** one idea per panel, about 40 words per panel at most, at least 4.5:1 text contrast, and nothing under 12 px at the published size. Count slots on the source before writing copy, because copy that overflows only shows once it's rendered.
