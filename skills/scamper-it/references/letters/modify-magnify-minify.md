# M — Modify / Magnify / Minify

**Definition.**
- **Modify:** change an attribute, such as meaning, colour, motion, sound, smell, form, shape, texture, tone or emotional register.
- **Magnify:** scale up. More, bigger, stronger, longer, more frequent, exaggerated, duplicated, with extra value added.
- **Minify:** scale down. Smaller, lighter, shorter, condensed, split up, less frequent, understated.

**The move.** Manipulate attributes and push them to extremes. Moving one attribute to its limit shows which attributes really matter and which are just habit. Cover all three directions. Running only "modify" is the most common way this letter gets wasted.

## Trigger questions
**Modify**
- What new twist could we give it? (Osborn)
- Could we change its meaning, colour, motion, sound, smell, form or shape? (Osborn)
- What if its emotional register changed: funny to earnest, calm to urgent, corporate to intimate?
- What should be emphasized, and what toned down?

**Magnify**
- What could be added? More time? Greater frequency? (Osborn)
- Stronger, higher, longer, thicker? (Osborn)
- Extra value? A "plus" ingredient? (Osborn)
- Duplicate it? Multiply it? Exaggerate it? (Osborn)
- What if it were 10× bigger, or happened 10× as often?
- What is the most exaggerated, over-the-top version?

**Minify**
- What could be subtracted? Smaller, condensed, miniature? (Osborn)
- Lower, shorter, lighter? (Osborn)
- Streamline it? Split it up? Understate it? (Osborn)
- What if it were a tenth of the size, the length, or the price?
- What is the smallest version that still delivers the core value?

## By domain
- **Content / marketing:**
  - Magnify: the "ultimate" 100-example guide, a stunt-scale claim, a daily series instead of a monthly one.
  - Minify: a one-sentence newsletter, a 6-second ad, a micro-course, a one-page version.
  - Modify: change the emotional register or the visual identity of a recurring series.
- **Writing:**
  - Magnify: raise the stakes; compress the timeline to 24 hours; turn a single detail into a symbol; exaggerate a character trait to its comic or tragic extreme.
  - Minify: a flash-fiction version, a six-word headline test, cut 30%.
  - Modify: shift tense, rhythm or sentence length.
- **Design / UI:**
  - Magnify: oversized hero type, one dominant colour, exaggerated whitespace.
  - Minify: progressive disclosure, a compact density mode, a check that the logo still reads at favicon size.
  - Modify: motion, texture, corner radius, the grid.
- **Business / process:**
  - Magnify: release frequency, customer touchpoints, the guarantee.
  - Minify: batch size, approval thresholds, meeting length, the number of tiers.

## Pitfalls
- Doing only Modify and skipping the extremes.
- Treating "bigger" as the only direction. Minify often wins, for example travel sizes or single-serve products.
- Making changes too small to test. If you can't tell the difference, it wasn't a real change.

## Verified or well-documented examples
- **Sony Walkman (1979).** Minify, plus Eliminate: the recording function and speaker of an existing portable recorder were dropped.
- Travel and mini sizes (Minify); supersizing (Magnify).
- Longer-playing VHS tape (Magnify), per Michalko.
- Avoid single-inventor "hot-dog bun" stories. There are many rival origin claims.
