# Selecting ideas and turning them into action

SCAMPER is good at producing many ideas but has no built-in way to judge them. Without a selection step, a session ends as a long list nobody acts on. Keep generating and judging strictly separate: finish generating before you start judging.

## Quick screens: pick one
- **NUF test** (default). Score each idea 1–5 on:
  - **New**: is it fresh to this audience or category?
  - **Useful**: does it serve the goal and the user?
  - **Feasible**: can it be done with these constraints?

  Show the scores in a small table, and add a one-line note wherever a score is surprising.
- **Impact × effort (2×2):**
  - quick wins: high impact, low effort;
  - big bets: high impact, high effort;
  - fill-ins: low impact, low effort;
  - money pits: low impact, high effort.

  Good for a team triaging a long list.
- **How–Now–Wow box** (COCD method):
  - *Now*: familiar and feasible;
  - *How*: original but not yet feasible;
  - *Wow*: original and feasible.

  This keeps bold ideas visible instead of letting them be quietly cut.

## Deeper evaluation: use for the shortlist
- **PMI (Plus / Minus / Interesting)**, from de Bono. Give real time to "Interesting", because that is where ideas get developed further.
- **PPCO (Pluses, Potentials, Concerns, Overcoming concerns)**, from the Creative Problem Solving tradition. Phrase each concern as "How might we overcome…?". This protects new ideas from early dismissal.
- **Weighted criteria matrix**, for higher-stakes choices. Possible criteria:
  - fit with the goal;
  - fit with the brand;
  - cost;
  - risk;
  - novelty;
  - time to test.
- **Dot voting**, for groups. Everyone votes silently before any discussion, which reduces anchoring. Optionally give each person separate "safe" and "bold" votes.
- **Six Thinking Hats**, for structured group critique of the top 2–3 ideas.

## Hybridize before you finalize
- Apply **Combine** to the ideas themselves. Pairs that come from different letters often merge well, for example a Substitute plus an Eliminate, or an Adapt plus a Reverse.
- Scale back a bold idea into a version you could test this month. This turns *How* ideas into *Wow* ones.
- Run a quick second SCAMPER pass on the top 1–3 ideas to sharpen them.

## Next-step template
For each chosen idea:

```
**<Concept in one line>** (from S + E)
- Why it could work: …
- Riskiest assumption: …
- Cheapest test: … (A/B headline, mock-up, landing-page smoke test, one pilot issue, a draft chapter, a two-week process pilot)
- Signal of success: …
- Owner / date: … (team contexts only)
```

Tests that fit each domain:
- **Copy:** A/B test the subject line or headline, or share a draft with five target readers.
- **Content:** a single pilot episode or post.
- **Design:** a clickable mock-up plus five usability sessions.
- **Process:** a time-boxed pilot against a baseline metric.
- **Fiction:** draft the changed scene and read it next to the original.
